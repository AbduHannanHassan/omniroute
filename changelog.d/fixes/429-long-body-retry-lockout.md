- **fix(sse):** a 429 whose body asks for a retry longer than the request-queue budget (Gemini answers an exhausted daily quota with `RetryInfo.retryDelay: "86399s"`) now locks that model on the connection, which the combo pre-dispatch gate and account selection already skip, instead of pausing the key's limiter for the whole wait — that pause made every request on the key sit out `maxWaitMs` and fail as a local 503 while the combo kept routing back to it. A limiter pause that outlasts a caller's remaining queue time now turns that call away at once instead of letting it wait the budget out, and the 429 header log no longer claims a pause it does not take.
