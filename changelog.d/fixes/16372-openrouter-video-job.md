- **fix(providers):** OpenRouter video models are callable again. `POST /api/v1/videos` answers 202 with a job id, and the synchronous OpenAI video handler reported that as success with an empty `data` array, so a combo stopped instead of trying its next target; `expired` jobs were polled until a misleading 504. `openrouter` now uses a submit→poll preset, the auth-gated `unsigned_urls` result is downloaded with the provider credential and re-served from `GET /api/v1/media/{id}`, and that URL is built from the caller's forwarded host rather than the bind address. Served media is scoped to the key that generated it and expires on the call-log retention window ([#16372](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/16372)) — thanks @LuisAlejandro
